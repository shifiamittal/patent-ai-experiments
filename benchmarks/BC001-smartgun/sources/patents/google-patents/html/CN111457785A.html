Abstract

An intelligent gun system capable of ensuring legal use and a use method thereof belong to the field of guns. The invention aims to solve the problem of illegal gun use. The intelligent gun is locked by the electric control fuse at ordinary times, when a gun holder is found to be possibly threatened, the control system identifies the gun by using the computer identification model, if the gun holder can use the gun legally, the control system sends an unlocking signal to the intelligent gun, the intelligent gun is verified and identified by using the computer identification model, if the identification result is consistent, the electric control fuse is opened by the intelligent gun, the gun holder can shoot the gun, the gun is immediately and automatically locked after shooting one gun, and the fuse is opened again after the next decrypted unlocking signal is received. The smart gun system does not access the internet to prevent illegal invasion. The control system may be divided into a fixed control system used in a fixed environment and an intelligent glasses type control system used in a non-fixed environment. The invention effectively prevents illegal gun use.

Technical Field
The invention relates to a gun and a using method thereof, in particular to an intelligent gun system capable of ensuring legal use and a using method thereof.
Background
At present, in parts of countries in Europe and America, people can legally hold guns, but illegal use situations of the guns occur occasionally. In the united states in particular, over thirty thousand people die of gunshots each year, causing great harm to the united states people and the united states society.
To the above situation, the technologist has designed various smart guns, as follows: the trigger condition for Aramatic ip1 is that the distance between the special watch worn by the gun holder and ip1 is less than 10 inches, and the password-activated ip1 must be reset every 8 hours, otherwise ip1 cannot be used; the Gun Box is a household safe for placing a Gun, and must be unlocked by fingerprints, and in addition, the safe is provided with a radio frequency identification device to ensure that the user can open the safe without scanning the fingerprints when wearing a ring with a radio frequency emission function to approach the safe; the RFID gun is internally provided with a signal receiving chip capable of receiving radio frequency and a storage chip for storing radio frequency comparison information of a user, the user must wear a ring with a radio frequency transmitting function, and the gun can be activated to transmit only when the distance between the ring and the gun is a few centimeters; the gun is characterized by comprising a fingerprint identification gun, wherein fingerprint identifiers are arranged at several parts of the gun, and the gun can be activated to shoot only when a set fingerprint is identified; zore X is an intelligent gun lock, and the gun lock can be unlocked to shoot only by inputting a password, so that unauthorized people can be prevented from unlocking the gun lock.
The intelligent firearms are technically used for ensuring that only an originally set gun holder can use the intelligent firearms, so that the intelligent firearms are prevented from being accidentally injured, stolen or robbed. However, these techniques of the intelligent gun cannot prevent the originally set gun holder from illegally using the gun to hurt others or suicide. And according to us 2004 statistical data: the number of people who die from suicide and murder accounts for 96 percent of the total number of people who die from gunshot, that is, the illegal use of guns is controlled to avoid more people from dying from non-lives.
Moreover, the legal gun use is complicated, and the legal requirements of gun holding and gun using in various states of the united states are different, so that some self-defenders cannot timely and effectively judge whether the gun can be used legally. The gun is used for self-defense, but the gun is delayed and uncertain, and the self-defense right can not be effectively exercised, thereby causing injury.
