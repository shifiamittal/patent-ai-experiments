FROZEN DISCLOSURE

The invention is a traceable smart firearm with:

1. Authorized-user biometric authentication, including fingerprint,
   facial recognition, palm-vein sensing or heart-rate-based authentication.

2. Authentication-dependent locking or unlocking that prevents discharge
   or operation by an unauthorized person.

3. Geolocation or geofencing that restricts firearm operation in
   prohibited locations.

4. Firearm tracking, traceability, biometric data or usage-log collection.

5. Transmission of activation or misuse information to a centralized or
   national database, potentially with law-enforcement alerts.

6. Classification of firearm use, such as self-defence, recreational use
   or another usage context.

7. An optional function that detects immediate danger through heart-rate
   or physiological information and conditionally unlocks the firearm.

OUT OF SCOPE

- AI threat assessment
- thermal or infrared sensing
- drone dock
- haptic trigger feedback
- kinetic charging
- marketing or “enhanced experience” benefits as standalone features
- friend/foe target selection as a target invention feature
- AI aiming or automatic target firing

CALCULATION

For F2–F8:

feature_points =
maximum feature weight × applicable feature evidence multiplier

For relationships:

relationship_points =
maximum relationship weight × applicable relationship multiplier

raw_score =
    domain_points
  + F2_points
  + F3_points
  + F4_points
  + F5_points
  + F2_to_F3_points
  + F4_to_F3_points
  + F5_to_F6_points
  + multi_primary_points
  + F6_points
  + F7_points
  + F8_points

penalized_score =
raw_score - applicable noise penalties

final_score =
minimum(
  applicable hard cap,
  maximum(0, minimum(100, round(penalized_score, 1)))
)

If no cap applies, applicable hard cap is 100.

   final score cap = 15

3. Holster or casing authentication without control of firearm

   final score cap = 20
