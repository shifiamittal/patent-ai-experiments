FROZEN DISCLOSURE

The invention is a traceable smart firearm with:

1. Authorized-user biometric authentication, including fingerprint,
   facial recognition, palm-vein sensing or heart-rate-based authentication.

2. Authentication-dependent locking or unlocking that prevents discharge
   or operation by an unauthorized person.

3. Geolocation or geofencing that restricts firearm operation in
   prohibited locations.

4. Firearm tracking, traceability, biometric data or usage-log collection.

5. Transmission of activation or misuse information to a centralized or
   national database, potentially with law-enforcement alerts.

6. Classification of firearm use, such as self-defence, recreational use
   or another usage context.

7. An optional function that detects immediate danger through heart-rate
   or physiological information and conditionally unlocks the firearm.
