# EXP-018A — Title/abstract extraction and gating defect repair

Baseline/champion comparator: EXP-013. Diagnostic comparator: EXP-016A. Source diagnosis: EXP-016A-D1 and the user's plain-English correction requirements.

Hypothesis: generalized repairs to authentication outcomes, functional relationships, technical architecture and storage senses improve ranking with the same evidence and arithmetic.

The pre-implementation change-plan.md specifies scope, risks and regression cases. manifest.json and outputs/validation.json record hashes. tests.json records all existing and new tests. outputs/full-ranking.csv is the complete ranking with evidence and score decomposition. outputs/correction-variants.json freezes sequential attribution before evaluation.

The ranking is committed before evaluation. D1 and the user request disclose prior diagnostic publication identities; permitted title/abstract inspection may reveal their corresponding content. No private mapping, GOLD movement file, restricted export or adjudication labels are read before that commit. This is a diagnosis-informed repair with evaluator separation, not a claim of zero prior knowledge.

Fixed: RUN-012's 3,238 identities and exact title/abstract input, benchmark and GOLD labels, all numeric weights/multipliers/penalties/caps, tie rules, metrics, retrieval universe and terminology source. No full text, claims, new retrieval or Precision@20. rs_v0.3 is unchanged.

Sequential attribution order: outcomes, relationships, mechanism, storage. Counts overlap and depend on order; they are not unique causal shares. Interpretation/score changes are separated from indirect rank displacement. Non-GOLD examples mean outside the known positive set, not adjudicated irrelevant.

Mechanism-general; validated on BC001 only.
